What we handle

Contract work, area by area

All prepared under Swedish law and written for the transaction in front of us, rather than adapted from a template.

How our contract law attorney handles disputes

Contract disputes usually come from vague wording, a broken obligation, late performance, or two readings of the same clause. We start with a proper assessment: the agreement itself, the correspondence around it, and the rules that apply — before we recommend a strategy rather than after.

Where a negotiated settlement is realistic we go for it, because it protects the commercial relationship and avoids the cost of litigating. Getting involved early is what usually keeps a disagreement from turning into court proceedings.

Business & commercial contract lawyer

Commercial agreements are the foundation of a working business relationship, and they should be drafted to reflect what each side is actually agreeing to. We assist with partnership contracts, shareholder arrangements, business transfer agreements and co-operation agreements, all built to comply with Swedish law.

We negotiate the provisions that decide who carries what: responsibilities, payment schedules, liability, confidentiality and how a dispute gets resolved. We also advise on franchise arrangements and corporate structuring.

Consultancy & employment contract lawyer

Employment and consultancy arrangements should set out each side's rights, responsibilities and expectations from the outset. We prepare and review employment contracts, consultant agreements, executive arrangements and independent contractor terms, drawn to meet Swedish employment standards while still protecting the business.

We also act in disputes arising from those agreements — pay and benefits, confidentiality, restrictive covenants and the circumstances of a termination — aiming for a commercially sensible outcome and, where a matter escalates, taking it through to representation.

Rental contracts & termination attorney

Rental agreements carry significant legal and financial obligations for landlord and tenant alike. We draft, review and negotiate both residential and commercial leases, covering the terms that matter in practice: unclear clauses, contractual obligations and the practical legal guidance that goes with them.

We also handle renewals, rent adjustments and the termination of a rental contract, and advise on the statutory tenant protections that apply whichever side of the agreement you are on.

Confidentiality agreements (NDAs)

A properly drafted confidentiality agreement matters as soon as sensitive business information, trade secrets or proprietary data are shared between parties. Ours define what is actually protected, what the recipient may do with it, what they may not, and what follows a breach — tailored to the relationship rather than lifted from a generic template.

We also act for clients enforcing an NDA where confidential information has been improperly disclosed: assessing the terms, the available remedies and what is realistically worth pursuing.

Contract review & drafting lawyer

A professional review identifies the legal risk before an agreement is signed. We go through it clause by clause for unclear language, provisions that are missing, terms weighted against you and anything that does not comply — and you get the amendments that would strengthen your position, not a list of observations.

Where we draft from scratch, the agreement covers rights and obligations, payment, liability, confidentiality, dispute resolution and termination, written for your transaction rather than adapted from a precedent.

What goes wrong

Common issues found in contract law agreements

Most disputes trace back to unclear drafting rather than anyone acting in bad faith.

01

Vague or inconsistent wording

The same thing called two different names in two clauses, or a term that both sides read their own way.

02

Obligations left out

What each party actually has to do, by when, and what happens if they do not.

03

Payment terms under-regulated

Timing, interest, set-off and what counts as an accepted invoice.

04

Liability left open

No cap, no carve-outs, or a limitation so broad a court would read it narrowly anyway.

05

Termination rights unclear

How either side gets out, on what notice, and what survives the exit.

06

Generic online templates

They rarely reflect Swedish law and never reflect your transaction. That combination is where most of this list comes from.

How it runs

Our contract service process

Four steps, in the same order every time, so you know what happens next and roughly what it costs.

  1. 01

    Initial legal consultation

    You describe the agreement and what you want from it. We ask the questions we need to understand the commercial position, not just the document.

  2. 02

    Contract review & assessment

    We read the agreement in full and set out the risks, the clauses working against you and the ones that are missing.

  3. 03

    Drafting & negotiation

    Amendments drafted and put to the other side, or a new agreement written from scratch. We handle the exchange with their advisers.

  4. 04

    Final review & execution

    A last pass over the agreed version before signature, so nothing has drifted between the negotiation and the document.
