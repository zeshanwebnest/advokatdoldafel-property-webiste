About the firm

A small firm, deliberately

Advantage is a Stockholm practice built on a simple principle: the lawyer you meet is the lawyer who runs your matter, from the first conversation to the last.

Small on purposeWe take on work at the rate we can run it properly

Our approach

Why we are built this way

Larger firms solve capacity problems by adding people to a matter. It works, but it has a cost: the person who understood your situation at the start is rarely the person arguing it at the end, and something is lost in every handover.

We took the opposite view. We stay small enough that the lawyer who assesses your matter is the one who runs it, and we take on work accordingly. That limits how much we can do at once. It also means you never have to explain your case twice.

Our practice areas were chosen for the same reason. Property, construction, commercial and employment law overlap constantly — a defective build is a construction problem, a contract problem and a property problem at the same time. Keeping them together means nobody has to co-ordinate three advisers who each see a third of the picture.

What we hold to

Working together

What happens after you get in touch

Four steps, in the same order every time. You will know after the second one whether there is something worth pursuing and what it would take.

  1. 01

    We listen

    You tell us what happened, in your own order. We ask the questions we need to understand the shape of it.

  2. 02

    We assess

    We read the documents properly and give you a candid view of the strengths, the weaknesses and the gaps.

  3. 03

    We propose

    A written scope: what we will do, in what order, on what fee basis, and what outcomes are realistic.

  4. 04

    We act

    From correspondence and negotiation through to proceedings, with updates when things move.
