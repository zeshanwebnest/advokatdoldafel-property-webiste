About the firm

A small firm, deliberately

Advantage is a Stockholm practice built around a simple idea: the lawyer you meet is the lawyer who runs your matter. No handovers, no layers, no learning your case twice.

We work across property, construction, commercial and employment law. Those areas overlap more than people expect — a defective build is a construction problem, a contract problem and often a property problem at once.
