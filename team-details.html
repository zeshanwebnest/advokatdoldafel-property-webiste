Partner

Peter Tagestam

Peter leads the firm's property and construction work. Most of his practice sits at the point where a building problem becomes a legal one.

He acts for buyers, sellers, landlords, developers and contractors — often on matters where a technical question and a contractual question have become tangled and nobody is sure which one to answer first.

A large part of his work involves defects discovered after completion. He is direct with clients about what these matters usually take: an independent expert who will address timing rather than just damage, prompt written notice, and a realistic view of what recovery looks like against the cost of pursuing it.

Where matters proceed, he prepares them as though they will be heard. In his experience that is the reason most of them are not.

Areas of work

  • Property transactions
  • Latent defect claims
  • Construction contracts
  • Delay and variation claims
  • Tenancy and leases
  • Litigation

How Peter works

  • The documents first Contracts, correspondence and reports read in full before any view is given.
