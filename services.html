How we work

From first call to conclusion

The same four stages, whatever the matter. You will always know which one you are in.

  1. 01

    First conversation

    You tell us what happened. We ask what we need to understand it and give you an honest read on where you stand — including when the answer is that there is nothing worth pursuing.

  2. 02

    Review and scope

    We go through the documents properly, identify what is missing, and set out in writing what we propose to do, on what basis, and at what cost.

  3. 03

    Strategy and preparation

    Evidence is gathered, the legal position is settled, and the claim or defence is put together. This is where matters are won, long before anyone sees a courtroom.

  4. 04

    Negotiation or proceedings

    Most matters resolve here. Where they do not, we take them forward and represent you throughout.
